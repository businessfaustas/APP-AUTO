Custom vehicle decals

Plotter-cut graphics, numbers, sponsor sets, lettering.

Get a quote

What it is

Plotter-cut decals: graphics, lettering, race numbers and full sponsor sets, cut from film rather than printed.

Sets are laid out for the car they're going on, and can be fitted in the workshop or supplied ready to fit with application tape.

Who it's for

Race and rally teams, clubs, and businesses that need lettering on a vehicle.

What's included

  • Plotter-cut graphics and lettering
  • Race and rally numbers
  • Full sponsor sets, cut to the car
  • Application tape and fitting, or supplied ready to fit

What it costs to get wrong

The cheap version

Cheap film cut from a low-resolution logo: ragged curves, lettering that cracks and shrinks, and adhesive that stays behind when the decals come off.

How we avoid it

Artwork is redrawn as vector before cutting, and decals are cut from cast film, so curves stay clean and the set comes off cleanly when it's time to change it.

Work

Questions

Can you cut a full sponsor set for a race car?

Yes. Send the sponsor list and logos with your car's make and model, and we'll lay the full set out on the car's outline before cutting.

Do you need vector files?

Vector files (SVG, AI, EPS or PDF) cut cleanest. If you only have an image, we redraw it and send you a proof before cutting.

Will the decals come off cleanly later?

Cast film comes off far more cleanly than cheap film. When the set needs changing, it lifts without dragging its adhesive with it.

Engraved badges

Custom badges, plaques and emblems.

Get a quote

What it is

Custom badges, plaques and emblems, engraved to your drawing or reproduced from an existing badge.

Tell us where the badge goes (grille, boot lid, engine bay, dashboard) and it's made and fixed to suit that spot.

Who it's for

Owners marking a special build, clubs, and restorers replacing a badge that's no longer made.

What's included

  • Badges, plaques and emblems made to your drawing
  • Reproduction of an existing badge
  • Fixing method matched to where it goes on the car

What it costs to get wrong

The cheap version

A badge that looks right in the hand and fails on the car: soft detail, a finish that dulls outdoors, a fixing that lets go at speed.

How we avoid it

The material and the fixing are chosen for where the badge will live, and you approve the drawing before anything is engraved.

Questions

What materials can you engrave?

It depends on where the badge will live. Tell us the location and the look you want, and we'll recommend a material along with the price.

Can you copy an original badge?

Usually, yes. Send clear photos from the front and at an angle, with measurements. If you have the original, even damaged, that's better.

How is the badge fixed to the car?

The fixing is chosen for the spot: adhesive for flat panels, pins or brackets where the original used them.

WHAT IT IS

Full vehicle projects

A full project is a colour change done as one job instead of five: the film, the chrome delete, the badges and the trim, planned together so every detail matches.

Handles, lights, badges and trim come off before any film goes on. The film wraps round each panel edge and, where the job includes them, into the door jambs.

WHO IT'S FOR

Enthusiasts and collectors changing the look of the whole car, and small fleets that want every vehicle identical.

COMPARISON

What it costs to get it wrong

THE CHEAP VERSION

Film trimmed in place around handles and badges, edges that stop a few millimetres short, calendered film in the recesses. From three metres it looks fine. Within a season the edges lift, the recesses pull back, and the knife lines are in your paint.

VS

AUTAPLATE

Parts come off, so there's no cutting on the paint and no visible edge at a handle. Cast film only, post-heated in every recess. The whole project is priced before the car comes in.

FAQ

Common questions

Not answered here? Call +370 62405636

01Which parts come off for a colour change?

Usually door handles, badges, lights and any trim that sits over a panel edge. The exact list for your car is in the written quote.

02Are door jambs wrapped?

Door jambs are an option, priced separately in the quote. If you want the car to look like its new colour with the doors open, they need doing.

03How long is the car in the workshop?

It depends on the car and on how much comes off. You get the timeline with the written price, before work starts.

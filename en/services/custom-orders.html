WHAT IT IS

Custom orders

Some jobs don't have a product code: a one-off graphic, a replacement piece for a car nobody makes parts for any more, something that exists only as a sketch. That's a custom order.

We take the brief from whatever you have (a drawing, a photo, the car itself), choose the material for where the piece will live, and make a test piece before the final one.

WHO IT'S FOR

Owners with an idea that doesn't fit a catalogue, and restorers who need one part nobody sells.

COMPARISON

What it costs to get it wrong

THE CHEAP VERSION

A one-off made from whatever material is on the shelf, cut to a guess at the size. It looks right in the photo and wrong on the car, and you pay twice: once for the part, once to have it made again.

VS

AUTAPLATE

The size is measured on the car. The material is chosen for where the piece lives: engine bay, exterior or cabin. You approve a test piece before the final run.

FAQ

Common questions

Not answered here? Call +370 62405636

01What counts as a custom order?

Anything outside the other five services: one-off graphics, replacement pieces, small runs for a club or a fleet. If you're not sure which service your job falls under, send it as a custom order.

02Can you work from a sketch or a photo?

Yes. A sketch, a photo or an old part is enough to start. We draw it up properly and send you a proof before anything is made.

03How is a one-off job priced?

After we've seen the brief and, where it matters, the car. You get a written price before work starts, and it doesn't change.

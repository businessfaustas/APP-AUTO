WHAT IT IS

OEM emission control labels

The emission control label under the bonnet is small, and on an original car it's the part people notice when it's missing or wrong.

We redraw the label from the original artwork (layout, typefaces, colours and size) and produce it ready to fit in the factory position.

WHO IT'S FOR

Restorers and collectors whose original label is faded, damaged or missing, and anyone preparing a car for judging or sale.

COMPARISON

What it costs to get it wrong

THE CHEAP VERSION

A label that's almost right: the wrong typeface, a guessed layout, colours a shade off. To anyone who knows the car it reads as fake, which is worse than no label at all.

VS

AUTAPLATE

The artwork is rebuilt from your original label, or from a photo of a correct one from the same model, and checked against it before printing.

FAQ

Common questions

Not answered here? Call +370 62405636

01Which makes and models can you reproduce?

Send a photo of your label, or of a correct label from the same model and year. If we can read it, we can redraw it.

02What do you need from me?

Clear photos of the original label, taken straight on in good light, plus the make, model and year. If your label is missing, a photo of a correct one from another car works.

03Will it match the original artwork?

That's the point of the service. You see a proof next to the original before anything is printed.

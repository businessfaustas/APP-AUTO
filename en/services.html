01 / 06

Custom orders

One-off work that doesn't fit a catalogue.

Some jobs don't have a product code: a one-off graphic, a replacement piece for a car nobody makes parts for any more, something that exists only as a sketch. That's a custom order.

We take the brief from whatever you have (a drawing, a photo, the car itself), choose the material for where the piece will live, and make a test piece before the final one.

What's included

  • Brief taken from a sketch, photo or the car itself
  • Material chosen for the job, not from stock on the shelf
  • Test piece before the final run
  • Material passport at handover

Who it's for

Owners with an idea that doesn't fit a catalogue, and restorers who need one part nobody sells.

02 / 06

Full vehicle projects

Colour change, chrome delete, badges and trim as one job.

A full project is a colour change done as one job instead of five: the film, the chrome delete, the badges and the trim, planned together so every detail matches.

Handles, lights, badges and trim come off before any film goes on. The film wraps round each panel edge and, where the job includes them, into the door jambs.

What's included

  • Full colour change in cast film
  • Chrome delete on window trim, grilles and badges
  • Badges and trim removed, refinished or replaced
  • Handles, lights and badges off before film goes on
  • Material passport at handover

Who it's for

Enthusiasts and collectors changing the look of the whole car, and small fleets that want every vehicle identical.

03 / 06

OEM emission control labels

Under-bonnet labels reproduced to original artwork.

The emission control label under the bonnet is small, and on an original car it's the part people notice when it's missing or wrong.

We redraw the label from the original artwork (layout, typefaces, colours and size) and produce it ready to fit in the factory position.

What's included

  • Artwork redrawn from the original label
  • Layout, type and colours matched to the factory part
  • Printed and cut to the original size
  • Ready to fit in the original position

Who it's for

Restorers and collectors whose original label is faded, damaged or missing, and anyone preparing a car for judging or sale.

04 / 06

Wrapping and livery design

Full and partial wraps, livery drawn on the actual panel geometry.

A full wrap covers the car. A partial wrap covers what the design needs: a roof, a bonnet, a set of stripes, a livery.

Livery is drawn on the measured panels of your car, not on a generic side view, so lines meet where the panels meet and nothing important lands on a door gap.

What's included

  • Full or partial wrap in cast film
  • Livery drawn on the measured panels of your car
  • Design proof on your car's outline before printing
  • Parts removed, edges wrapped round
  • Material passport at handover

Who it's for

Owners who want a new look or a livery, race and rally teams, and small businesses marking their vehicles.

05 / 06

Custom vehicle decals

Plotter-cut graphics, numbers, sponsor sets, lettering.

Plotter-cut decals: graphics, lettering, race numbers and full sponsor sets, cut from film rather than printed.

Sets are laid out for the car they're going on, and can be fitted in the workshop or supplied ready to fit with application tape.

What's included

  • Plotter-cut graphics and lettering
  • Race and rally numbers
  • Full sponsor sets, cut to the car
  • Application tape and fitting, or supplied ready to fit

Who it's for

Race and rally teams, clubs, and businesses that need lettering on a vehicle.

Custom vehicle decals →

06 / 06

Engraved badges

Custom badges, plaques and emblems.

Custom badges, plaques and emblems, engraved to your drawing or reproduced from an existing badge.

Tell us where the badge goes (grille, boot lid, engine bay, dashboard) and it's made and fixed to suit that spot.

What's included

  • Badges, plaques and emblems made to your drawing
  • Reproduction of an existing badge
  • Fixing method matched to where it goes on the car

Who it's for

Owners marking a special build, clubs, and restorers replacing a badge that's no longer made.

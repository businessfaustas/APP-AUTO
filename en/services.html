PRICING

How a price gets fixed

  1. 01

    Inspection or photos

    Send photos through the quote form or bring the car in. Some jobs can be priced from photos. A full project needs the car.

  2. 02

    Written price, with the work listed

    The price comes in writing, with the film, the design and the work listed. If it isn't on the list, it isn't in the price.

  3. 03

    The price doesn't move mid-job

    Once you've agreed, the price is fixed. If we find something unexpected, we stop and tell you. Nothing is added without your yes.

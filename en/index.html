MATERIALS

Cast.
ORAFOL.
Post-heated.

CASTWhat cast film is

Cast film is made by casting, so it carries no built-in tension: it doesn't shrink, lies well into curves and holds its colour for years. Cheaper calendered film shrinks back over time and lifts out of recesses.

ORAFOLThe film we use

We work with cast films from the German maker ORAFOL. Every job uses the same proven material, so colour and quality stay consistent.

HEATEDWhy recesses are post-heated

Stretched film wants its old shape back. Post-heated, it stays where it was laid.

ARRIVAL TO HANDOVER

One car. Three stages.

01

Before The brief

Before anything is touched, the job is written down: the film, the colour, which parts come off, and the price. That price doesn't move.

02

During The work

Handles, lights, badges and trim come off. Film goes onto clean, bare panels, round every edge, and is post-heated in each recess so it can't pull back.

03

After The result

Parts go back where they came from, the car is checked over, and you collect it with care advice for the film on it.

MATERIALS

Cast.

CASTWhat cast film is

Poured, not stretched, so it carries no tension and doesn't shrink. Cheaper calendered film lifts out of recesses within weeks.

70 µmWhy 70 microns

Thin enough to lie into curves, thick enough not to crease while it's laid.

HEATEDWhy recesses are post-heated

Stretched film wants its old shape back. Post-heated, it stays where it was laid.

ABOUT — KLAIPĖDA

The workshop

Not a body shop with a vinyl side line. A wrapping and personalisation workshop.

THE WORKSHOP

What this place is

AUTAPLATE is a wrapping and personalisation workshop in Klaipėda. Wraps, livery, decals, OEM labels and engraved badges are the whole job here, not a side line to bodywork.

Many of the people who contact us have already paid for one cheap wrap. They want to see how we handle the parts nobody photographs: the jambs, the recesses, the edge behind a handle.

Where we are

The workshop is in Klaipėda, Lithuania. If you want to see a car mid-job, call first and we'll tell you when to come.

The workspace

Cars are worked on indoors with panels bare and the parts that came off laid out beside them. That halfway stage is the one a cheap shop never shows you. Here you can ask to see it.

THREE RULES

Three rules,
explained

01

Cast film only

Calendered film is cheaper and fine for a flat sign. On a car it shrinks back, lifts in recesses and cracks as it ages. We apply cast film only, so there is no cheaper version of your job to swap in quietly.

02

Parts come off the car

Film trimmed around a handle or badge ends in a visible edge, and cutting in place puts a blade next to your paint. With the part off, the film wraps round the edge of the panel. Everything goes back where it came from.

03

The price is fixed before work starts

You get the price in writing before work starts. If we find something we didn't expect, we stop and tell you before touching it. Nothing is added to the bill without your agreement.

LIMITS

What we won't do

  • 01Apply calendered film, even on a panel you'll never look at.
  • 02Cut film on your paint around handles, badges or lights.
  • 03Change the price after work has started without asking you first.
  • 04Hand a car back without its material passport.

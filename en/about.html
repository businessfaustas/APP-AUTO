THE WORKSHOP

What this place is

AUTAPLATE is a wrapping and personalisation workshop in Klaipėda. Wraps, livery, decals, OEM labels and engraved badges are the whole job here, not a side line to bodywork.

HOW WE WORK

Three principles

01

ORAFOL cast film only

Calendered film is cheaper, but on a car it shrinks back, lifts in recesses and cracks as it ages. We apply ORAFOL cast film only, so there is no cheaper version to swap in quietly.

02

Design for your car

Graphics are drawn on the measured panels of your car. You see and approve the proof before anything is printed, so nothing on the car comes as a surprise.

03

A written price first

You get the price in writing before work starts. If we find something we didn't expect, we stop and tell you before touching it. Nothing is added to the bill without your agreement.

LIMITS

What we won't do

  • Calendered filmNot even on a panel you'll never look at.
  • Printing before you approveYou see the design on your car's outline first.
  • Price changes mid-jobNothing is added to the bill without your agreement.
